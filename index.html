01. about

I'm a Digital Agriculture student at Olds College — my time is spent in the field or in the office analyzing the data. I spent last summer at the Olds College Centre for Innovation collecting data via drones, soil probes, GPS equipment, and many more sensors. But my favorite part is then making sense of all that data. That gap between unusable raw field data and something actually useful is what drives most of my work.

Right now I'm building RETA, an open-source toolkit for cleaning agricultural geo-spatial data such as soil and yield data, and its machine learning extension RETA_ML, which uses graph neural networks to detect spatial outliers in field sensor data.

Based in Carstairs, Alberta.
